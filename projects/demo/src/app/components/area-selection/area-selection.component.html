Area selection The library marks a region and says what is in it. Everything else on this page — the key that deletes, the confirm dialog, the toolbar — is application code.

[enableAreaSelection] arms the marquee in the editor: drag from an empty cell and every widget the rectangle touches is marked. The dashboard reports the settled rectangle through (areaSelectionChange), names what it caught through selectedWidgetIds(), and removes it when the app calls deleteSelectedWidgets(). No keystroke is bound by the library, which is why the binding below is this page's to choose.
